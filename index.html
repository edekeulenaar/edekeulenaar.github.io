Emillie de Keulenaar

I research how conflicts transform in online information spaces, and how conflict resolution methods from peace studies, the philosophy of dialogue and democratic theory can be operationalised into content moderation, social media and AI affordances.

Postdoctoral researcher at the University of Amsterdam. Research associate at the University of Manchester. Research consultant to the UN Department of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs since 2020, and co-founder of the Open Intelligence Lab.
